SARMIS

SARMIS is a private assistant that one person, Marius Manea, built for himself and runs on his own home server. It is not a product or a public service, and it has no other users.

With the owner's consent, it reads his own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive so that it can remind him of things, answer his questions about his own messages and files, and draft replies or calendar changes that he approves one by one.

How it handles that data is described in the privacy policy.
